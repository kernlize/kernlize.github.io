Messaging

Described, not ranked.

Private messaging in kernl works across several providers. None of them is "the best". The shell tells you plainly what each one protects, and you pick the one the person you are writing to can read.

The words

What the rows mean.

Forward secrecy
If your keys leak today, messages from before today stay unreadable.
Heals after a key leak
Once both of you keep writing, a leaked key stops being useful to whoever has it.
Metadata protection
How much someone watching the network learns about who talks to whom and when. "Partial" means the sender and timing are hidden but delivery is not.
Other person needs this app
Whether your correspondent must use an app that speaks the same provider.
Experimental
The provider is still changing and may lose history between versions. kernl says so before you enable it.

Applets see one messaging surface that spans every provider you enabled. The choice of provider stays with you, is shown on every conversation, and can be changed. More in the documentation.

kernl is alpha software, MIT licensed, built in the open.

The person at the keyboard is the only principal who matters.
